COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers.
No jargon needed.

Does Otto issue audited financial statements?

No. An independent auditor must conduct a separate audit and issue the auditor's report. Otto's financial statement outputs are drafts.

Can an external accountant review the UFS?

Yes, under a separate professional engagement. Otto's integrated review workflow is planned, not yet implemented.

Does audit exemption mean no accounting standards apply?

No. Audit exemption and financial reporting obligations are separate. Directors remain responsible for appropriate financial statements.

Can Otto read scanned bank statements?

The current development engine reads text-based PDFs, CSV and Excel. Image-only scans are not automatically OCR-converted.

Does Otto file tax or ACRA returns?

No. Corporate tax, GST, IRAS and ACRA/XBRL filings are excluded from the current offering.

Is Otto commercially launched?

Not yet. M0 stabilisation, Mac acceptance testing and professional accounting review are still in progress.
