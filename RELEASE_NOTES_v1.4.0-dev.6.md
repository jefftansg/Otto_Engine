# Otto Financial Report Engine v1.4.0-dev.6

23 November 2026. Development candidate adding direct client replies with per-query dropdowns and manual entry. No final release or financial/template approval.

Enter pending transaction and general-question replies directly in Otto using Reply category / Reply option, Custom category / reply and Client explanation. Save client replies records facts and prior-answer history with the recorder name; no workbook is required. General Not sure replies remain open. Known simple transaction reply categories become proposals for the existing separate accounting-category approval. Ambiguous loans, owner funding, transfers and custom replies do not automatically choose financial accounts or create transfers. Bank rows, amounts, dates and journals remain unchanged until the existing relevant financial approval.

Direct and Excel replies share storage, conflict checks and history. The Excel workbook also includes the expanded reply choices, and uncertain replies remain Needs answer. See docs/36_DIRECT_CLIENT_REPLIES.md and the current Quick Start for usage and validation.
